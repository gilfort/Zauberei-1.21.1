# Magiccloth Armor

The Witchwood school uniform. All pieces are animated with GeckoLib and repaired with leather.

| Piece | Item id | Alternative style | Ars Nouveau variant |
|---|---|---|---|
| Hood | `zauberei:magiccloth_helmet` | `zauberei:magiccloth_helmet_alt` | `zauberei:magiccloth_helmet_perks` |
| Robe | `zauberei:magiccloth_chestplate` | `zauberei:magiccloth_chestplate_alt` | `zauberei:magiccloth_chestplate_perks` |
| Pants | `zauberei:magiccloth_leggings` | `zauberei:magiccloth_leggings_alt` | `zauberei:magiccloth_leggings_perks` |
| Boots | `zauberei:magiccloth_boots` | `zauberei:magiccloth_boots_alt` | `zauberei:magiccloth_boots_perks` |

- **Defense** (helmet / chestplate / leggings / boots): 1 / 3 / 2 / 1, enchantability 15.
- **Alternative style:** the `_alt` pieces use their own model and texture.
- **Ars Nouveau:** the `_perks` pieces only exist when Ars Nouveau is installed. Perk slots: hood and boots
  2 (tier one, two), robe 3 (one, one, three), pants 3 (one, two, two).
- **Tags:** all pieces are in `#zauberei:magiccloth_armor` and the vanilla `#minecraft:head_armor` /
  `chest_armor` / `leg_armor` / `foot_armor` tags, which makes them enchantable like vanilla armor.

The Magiccloth items can be hidden from the creative tab and JEI/EMI with `enable_magical_armor` (see
[Configuration](Configuration)).
